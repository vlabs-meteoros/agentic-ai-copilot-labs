# Incident facts: KaveriPay login outage (internal - for the customer email)

* **Product:** KaveriPay, Kaveri Systems' online payroll service. About 1,200 business customers use it.
* **When:** Friday 14 November 2026, from 09:40 to 12:15 IST (2 hours 35 minutes).
* **What customers saw:** they could not sign in to KaveriPay, so they could not run or approve payroll.
* **Cause:** a configuration change to our sign-in service. It was not a cyber attack.
* **Data:** no customer or employee data was lost or exposed.
* **Fix:** we reversed the change at 12:15 IST. We have added an automatic check that tests sign-in before any configuration change goes live.
* **Payroll impact:** payroll runs scheduled for that morning were delayed. All of them were completed by 15:00 IST the same day, and every salary was credited on time.
* **Goodwill credit:** every affected customer gets a 10% credit on their November invoice. It is applied automatically; customers don't need to do anything.
* **Help:** support@kaverisystems.example or 1800-200-4357 (toll free, 24x7).
* **Sender:** Ananya Rao, Head of Customer Success, Kaveri Systems.
